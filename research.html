Atomistic Machine LearningAtomistic Machine Learning — 原子体系机器学习

Atomistic machine learning is machine learning for systems made of atoms — inorganic materials, organic molecules, and drugs. It learns their physics and chemistry from data, spanning graph neural networks (GNNs), machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) that bring quantum-mechanical accuracy to simulations orders of magnitude faster, and generative models for crystal structures. We develop foundation models (e.g. CHGNet) and libraries (e.g. MatGL), the datasets and benchmarks that train and test them, and the understanding needed to trust them — systematic errors, cross-functional transfer, thermodynamics, and scalable distributed inference. Atomistic Machine Learning（原子体系机器学习）是面向由原子构成的体系——无机材料、有机分子、药物分子等——的机器学习，从数据中学习其物理与化学规律，涵盖图神经网络（GNN）、以接近量子化学的精度将原子模拟加速数个量级的机器学习势函数（MLIPs），以及晶体结构生成模型等。我们研发原子体系基础模型（如 CHGNet）与开源库（如 MatGL），构建训练与评测所需的数据集和基准，并深入理解其可靠性——系统性误差、跨泛函迁移、热力学性质以及大规模分布式推理。

Quantum Materials & Chemical Simulations for End-to-end Materials DiscoveryQuantum Materials & Chemical Simulations for End-to-end Materials Discovery — 面向端到端材料发现的量子化学计算

Quantum materials and chemical simulations — such as density functional theory (DFT) — provide the physical ground truth for our models and agents. We couple them with machine learning and statistical mechanics to explore large chemical spaces, explain the mechanisms behind materials behavior, and power end-to-end materials discovery, with past work on battery cathodes, solid-state ionic conductors, and crystal structure prediction. 以密度泛函理论（DFT）为代表的量子化学计算，为我们的模型与智能体提供物理上的“真值”。我们将其与机器学习、统计力学相结合，探索广阔的化学空间、揭示材料性能背后的机理，支撑端到端的材料发现（End-to-end Materials Discovery）；已有工作涵盖电池正极材料、固态离子导体与晶体结构预测等。

04

Agent-driven Autonomous End-to-end DiscoveryAgent-driven Autonomous End-to-end Discovery — 智能体驱动的自主端到端发现

Our ultimate goal is AI scientists that carry out discovery end to end: reading the literature, forming hypotheses, designing and running computational workflows with atomistic ML and quantum simulations, analyzing the results, and deciding what to try next — iterating until they find something new. Automated and robotic labs are one of the tools such an agent can call to validate its predictions, but the core of this direction is the agent's own scientific reasoning and decision-making across the whole research cycle. This is a core direction of the new lab at Fudan AI³. 我们的终极目标是让 AI 科学家端到端地完成科学发现：检索与阅读文献、提出科学假设、借助 Atomistic ML 与量子化学计算设计并执行计算流程、分析结果并自主决定下一步，不断迭代直至获得新的发现。自动化 / 机器人实验平台是智能体可以调用、用于验证预测的工具之一，而这一方向的核心在于智能体贯穿整个科研流程的科学推理与决策能力。这是实验室在复旦 AI³ 研究院的核心发展方向之一。
