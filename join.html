I'm Bowen Deng, currently a postdoc at MIT. In early 2027 I will join the Institute of AI Innovation and Industry (AI³) at Fudan University as an Assistant Professor and PhD advisor, and start the Atomistic Intelligence Lab.

We work on AI for Science, combining scientific AI agents, atomistic machine learning (e.g. GNNs and ML interatomic potentials), quantum materials and chemical simulations (e.g. DFT), and end-to-end agentic materials research to create reliable AI scientists and accelerate the development of new materials.

If you are excited about AI for Science — scientific AI agents, atomistic machine learning, or end-to-end agentic materials research — I'd love to hear from you.
