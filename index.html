AI for Science & Scientific AI Agents AI for Science 与科学智能体

The Atomistic Intelligence Lab at Fudan University's AI³ Institute designs and builds AI systems that do science. We combine scientific AI agents, atomistic machine learning, quantum materials and chemical simulations, and end-to-end agentic materials research to build reliable AI scientists and accelerate the development of new materials. 复旦大学 AI³ 研究院原子智能实验室（Atomistic Intelligence Lab）致力于 AI for Science 研究。课题组围绕 Scientific AI Agents（科学智能体）、Atomistic Machine Learning（原子体系机器学习）、Quantum Materials & Chemical Simulations（量子化学计算）与End-to-end Agentic Materials Research（端到端智能体材料研究）相结合，构建可靠的 AI 科学家，加速新材料的研发。
